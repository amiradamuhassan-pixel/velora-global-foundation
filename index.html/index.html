Our Voices, Our Commitment

01. Executive Chairperson

ENGLISH MESSAGE

Leadership is not simply about holding a position; it is about accepting responsibility, inspiring others and creating meaningful opportunities for positive change. At VELORA GLOBAL FOUNDATION, our vision is to build a strong and compassionate organisation that serves humanity, supports education, empowers young people and contributes to the development of our communities. Together, we can turn our shared vision into meaningful action and create a lasting legacy for future generations.

SAƘO A HAUSA

Shugabanci ba wai kawai rike mukami ba ne, alhaki ne, amana ne da kuma sadaukar da kai wajen taimakon al'umma. A matsayina na shugabar VELORA GLOBAL FOUNDATION, burina shi ne ganin mun gina gidauniya mai karfi, wadda za ta taimaki marasa galihu, inganta ilimi, karfafa matasa da kuma kawo ci gaba a cikin al'umma. Ina kira ga kowa da kowa da mu hada kai domin cimma wannan buri namu na alheri.

02. Vice Chairperson

ENGLISH MESSAGE

I strongly believe that every meaningful achievement begins with a vision, determination and collective effort. As Vice Chairperson of VELORA GLOBAL FOUNDATION, I am committed to supporting our leadership, promoting unity, strengthening collaboration and contributing to programmes that create positive social impact. Our mission is bigger than individuals. It is about humanity, opportunity and building a future where everyone has the chance to make a difference.

SAƘO A HAUSA

Na yi imani cewa duk wani babban ci gaba yana farawa ne da hangen nesa, jajircewa da hadin kai. A matsayina na mataimakin shugaba, ina da kudurin taimakawa wajen tabbatar da manufofin wannan gidauniya, karfafa hadin kai, samar da kyakkyawar alaka da sauran kungiyoyi da kuma aiwatar da ayyukan da za su amfani al'umma. Babban burinmu shi ne mu zama sanadin alheri da canji mai kyau a rayuwar mutane.

03. Chief Programmes Director

ENGLISH MESSAGE

A vision becomes meaningful when it is transformed into action. As Chief Programmes Director, I believe in developing and coordinating initiatives that address real community needs, particularly in education, youth development, humanitarian support and community empowerment. SAƘO A HAUSA

Hangen nesa yana da amfani ne idan aka mayar da shi aiki na zahiri. A matsayina na Daraktar shirye-shirye, na kuduri aniyar taimakawa wajen tsara shirye-shirye da ayyukan da za su magance matsalolin al'umma, musamman a fannin ilimi, ci gaban matasa, agajin jin kai da bunkasa al'umma. Muna son ganin ayyukanmu sun zama sanadin samun damammaki da ingantacciyar rayuwa ga al'umma.
